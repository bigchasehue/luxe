HERITAGE & MASTERY

Crafting Spaces
You’ll Love Forever.

Founded with an uncompromising devotion to architectural clarity, LUXE. fuses traditional hand-joinery with cutting-edge parametric design. Every creation is born from slow craftsmanship, honoring the natural grain of sustainably harvested European timber and the tactile richness of Italian top-grain leathers.

We believe an environment is not merely inhabited; it is felt. Our bespoke pieces serve as functional art, bringing emotional resonance, warmth, and enduring luxury to discerning private residences worldwide.

THE FOUNDER

Amara Okafor, Founder & Creative Director

Amara founded LUXE. after two decades studying furniture, architecture, and material culture across Lagos, Milan, and London. Her Nigerian heritage and European atelier training shape every collection: warm, expressive pieces with rigorous proportions, honest materials, and a deep respect for the way people gather at home. From our Lagos studio, she leads a design team committed to making collectible furniture feel personal, livable, and enduring.
